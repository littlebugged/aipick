# About AIPick

## Our Mission

AIPick is a handpicked directory of the best AI tools for every task. We help people discover the right AI tools for writing, coding, design, video, marketing, research, and more.

In a world flooded with AI products launching daily, it's hard to find what actually works. We cut through the noise and curate tools that are worth your time.

## What We Do

- **Curate** — We research and verify each tool before listing it
- **Categorize** — Organized by use case so you find what you need fast
- **Update** — We keep listings current as tools evolve

## For Tool Makers

Want to get your AI tool in front of thousands of users actively searching for solutions? [Submit your tool](/submit) for listing in our directory.

We offer:
- **Free listings** — Basic listing with name, description, and link
- **Featured listings** — Priority placement with highlighted badge
