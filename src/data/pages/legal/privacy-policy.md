# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 4, 2026*

## Who We Are

AIPick ("we", "us", "our") operates the website [https://aipick.de5.net](https://aipick.de5.net). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our site.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Submission Form**: When you submit an AI tool via our form, we collect the tool name, URL, description, category, and your email address. This information is sent directly through your email client — we do not store it on any server.

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Cookies**: We use essential cookies to remember your preferences (such as dark/light mode).
- **Analytics**: We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand how visitors use our site (page views, referral sources). No personally identifiable information is collected.
- **Advertising**: We display third-party advertisements (currently via Adsterra) which may use cookies to serve relevant ads. These ads are loaded in isolated iframes and cannot access your browsing data on our site.

## How We Use Your Information

- To display and improve our AI tools directory
- To process tool submissions
- To display relevant advertisements
- To analyze site usage and improve user experience

## Third-Party Services

### Advertising (Adsterra)
We use Adsterra to display advertisements. Adsterra may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your interests. Their ad content is loaded in sandboxed iframes that cannot access or modify our website's content.

- [Adsterra Privacy Policy](https://www.adsterra.com/privacy-policy/)

### Hosting (Cloudflare)
Our site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect minimal technical data (IP addresses, browser type) for security and performance purposes.

- [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

## Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

| Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|----------|
| Essential | Theme preference (dark/light mode) | Persistent |
| Advertising | Ad targeting and frequency capping | Varies by provider |

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. The site will continue to function without them.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of advertising cookies via your browser settings
- Contact us with any privacy concerns

## Data Retention

We do not store any user data on our servers. Submissions are sent via email, and any analytics data is aggregated and anonymized.

## Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
