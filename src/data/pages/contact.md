# Contact Us

## Get in Touch

We'd love to hear from you. Whether you have a question, feedback, or a business inquiry, reach out and we'll get back to you.

## Common Reasons to Contact Us

### Submit a Tool
Want to list your AI tool in our directory? Use our [submission form](/submit) or email us directly.

### Report an Issue
Found incorrect information about a listed tool? Let us know and we'll update it.

### Business & Partnerships
Interested in featured listings, advertising, or partnerships? Email us with details about your proposal.

### Feedback
Have suggestions to improve AIPick? We're always looking to make the directory more useful.

## Response Time

We typically respond within 24-48 hours on business days.
